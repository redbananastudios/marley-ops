## intro

Welcome to Marley Ops, the app for every job you do with us. This video shows your day, step by step. The day always flows the same way. Open the app. See today's jobs. Open a job to get the route and the items. If there is a problem, take a photo and a note. And you finish with both of you signing.

## part-1

Part one. Start your day. Open the app from your home screen. Your jobs are in a list, and today is at the top. Look at the week strip. A red number on a day means you have jobs that day.

## part-2

Part two. Open a job. Tap the job card. You will see where to go, who you are working with, and which van. Then read the item list. If an item says not moving, leave it in the house.

## part-3

Part three. Get there. Tap Directions on an address card, and your maps app opens with the route ready. Need the customer? Tap Call at the bottom of the page.

## part-4

Part four. When you arrive. Look for a yellow banner at the top of the job. It means the contract is not signed yet. Tap Collect signature now, then hand the customer your phone. They sign with a finger. Done. No yellow banner? Good. It is already signed. Start the job.

## part-5

Part five. A problem? Write it down. Damage, or you cannot get in? Open Crew notes and photos on the job. Add a photo of the problem. Write a short note. Then tap Save note. This is private. Only the office sees it, never the customer. And remember. Never argue on site. Save a note, then call the office.

## part-6

Part six. A good moment? Get it on camera. A tidy load, or the team working hard? Tap the round red camera button. This one is different. Choose Photo, Video or Voice. The van, the outside and the team are always fine. Taking pictures inside their home? Ask the customer first. Then tap, Customer's OK'd it. Not sure? Tap, Keep internal only. The office picks the best bits for our posts.

## part-7

Part seven. Finishing the job. Tap Complete job. Did anything break or go wrong? Write it in the box. Tell the truth. It protects you. The customer signs on your screen, then you sign. The customer gets a certificate by email. Job done.

## part-8

Part eight. The job sheet. Tap Job sheet on any move to get a one page brief. Addresses, van, items. It never shows prices, so it is safe to leave in the cab.

## part-9

Part nine. Set up your phone. On the My jobs page, find Your device at the bottom. Add the app to your home screen. Turn on job alerts, so your phone buzzes when you are put on a job. And set up face or fingerprint sign in. No password to type.

## recap

That is your day. Open the app. See your jobs. Get the route. Record any problem. Finish with both signatures. And if anything on a job looks wrong, call the office before you set off.
