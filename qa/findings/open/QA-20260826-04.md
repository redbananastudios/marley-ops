## Summary
A non-default brand's storage INVOICE email states MarleyMoves Ltd's company number directly beside the other brand's trading name, and drops the VAT number entirely — so the one document a customer keeps for a VAT-bearing charge asserts a legal identity that is not the registered company. The same brand's quote and balance-invoice emails render the correct disclosure, so two money documents from one brand state two different legal identities, and the wrong one is the invoice.

## Repro
1. Precondition: a storage let belonging to a non-default brand (e.g. pitmans) reaches its billing date.
2. `raiseDueStorageInvoices` → `emailStorageInvoice` resolves the let's brand (`lib/storage/raise-storage-invoices.ts:206`).
3. Read the rendered email footer.
4. Expected: the same legal line the brand's other emails carry — "<Brand> is a trading name of MarleyMoves Ltd. Company No. 15914266. VAT 520 2213 58."
5. Actual: "<Brand> · Company No. 15914266 · <brand phone>" — the number reads as the brand's own, MarleyMoves Ltd is named nowhere, VAT number absent.

## Evidence
`lib/comms/email-brand.ts:348` — `themedDarkFooter` hardcodes the company number next to whatever brand name it is passed, and never renders `t.footerMetaHtml`, which is the field carrying the correct trading-name disclosure. `themedEmailShell` (the path quote + balance-invoice emails take) DOES render `footerMetaHtml`, which is why those two surfaces are correct and this one is not.

## Suspected cause
`themedDarkFooter` predates the brand layer and was never brought onto the `footerMetaHtml` contract when `themedEmailShell` was. Fix is to render `t.footerMetaHtml` in `themedDarkFooter` exactly as the shell does, rather than composing an identity line from a hardcoded constant.

Filed `risky`: it changes the legal text on a VAT-bearing customer document, so it is a Peter decision and must not be auto-fixed. Not yet reachable in production — no non-default brand has a storage let — which is why it is filed rather than hot-fixed, but it becomes live the moment one does.
