## Summary
Changing a move date from `/bookings` inside the 7-day window releases the old
appointment and books a brand-new one (cancel+rebook, not an in-place edit) —
but the crew/vehicle assignment does not carry over to the new appointment,
and the previously-assigned crew member gets no notice at all that the job
they were expecting has disappeared from their day.

## Repro
1. Admin login, marker lead with an accepted quote, deposit paid, and a
   removal appointment scheduled for tomorrow with a crew member + vehicle
   assigned via `appointment_assignments`.
2. `/bookings` → the lead's row → **"Change date"** → fill both
   `input[type="datetime-local"]` fields with a new date (any date — the
   original was inside 7 days so the "inside window" branch fires
   regardless of the new date) → tick the acknowledgement checkbox →
   **"Release old date & rebook"**.
3. Toast: "Original date released and the new date booked." `/bookings`
   correctly shows a **"No crew — allocate"** chip and the "TO ALLOCATE"
   tile increments — the office side is told.
4. Log in as the crew member who was assigned to the OLD appointment,
   go to `/my-jobs`.
5. Expected: something tells this crew member their previously-scheduled
   job moved or was cancelled (matches the pattern already used elsewhere,
   e.g. survey reschedule emails/SMS).
   Actual: the job silently vanishes — `/my-jobs` reads "Nothing assigned
   to you yet." No cancellation notice, no "your job moved" message,
   nothing. Visiting the old appointment's URL directly 404s cleanly (no
   stale-data leak), but there is no positive signal of any kind.

## Evidence
Live run (admin role-agent, staging, run20399), 2026-08-23T04:2x:
- Old appointment `a92c586d-ee4e-4955-9a61-7e8f5115a1d7`: `status`
  `scheduled → cancelled`, `starts_at`/`ends_at` unchanged.
- New appointment `2284931e-240f-43e6-9ebe-03ae9b92e523`: new row,
  `status='scheduled'`, same `lead_id`/`client_id`, new date.
- `appointment_assignments` for the OLD appointment: still holds both
  rows (staff `5151ec5e-6ff9-4d47-b324-3e855fa2efb5`, vehicle
  `a418698e-3935-4102-80f1-fb4f48207191`) — orphaned on a cancelled
  appointment.
- `appointment_assignments` for the NEW appointment: **0 rows**.
- `/my-jobs` for that crew login: "Nothing assigned to you yet."
- `refund_queue`: 0 rows (correct — nothing was retainable pre-confirmation,
  not part of this finding).

## Suspected cause
`app/actions/booking-change.ts` `changeBookingDateAction` (the
Payments-Policy-v2 cancel+rebook path, ~line 381 area per a prior audit
note on a related no-op) creates the new appointment row but never copies
`appointment_assignments` from the old one, and nothing in this action (or
downstream) sends the previously-assigned crew member any comms about the
old appointment being cancelled.

## Why this is `risky`, not `safe-fix`
This is a named, already-tracked product gap — `AGENTS.md`'s 2026-08-08
"NOT fixed — needs Peter's decision" list states: *"crew are never told a
job they're on was cancelled or moved more than ~2 days out."* This run
shows the gap also applies to the newer Payments Policy v2 change-date
flow (a different code path from the older removal-cancel flow the note
was originally about). The correct fix is a product decision, not a pure
bug fix: should the assignment auto-carry-over to the new date (risk: crew
committed to a van/slot that might not actually be free that day, silently
double-booking them), or should it deliberately drop (current behaviour)
but with an explicit "your job on the old date was cancelled" notice to
the crew member either way? Either answer touches comms-sending policy —
per the findings protocol, comms sending is `risky` by definition, and
"when unsure, risky" applies regardless.

## Fix shipped (2026-08-23, PR #54) — direction (b)

Peter's standing call was NOT to reverse the documented drop: the office
re-allocates on the Job Board, and `changeBookingDateAction` says so in a
comment, so carrying the assignment forward would have silently reversed a
deliberate product decision (and risked committing crew to a van/slot that
may not be free). The defect is the SILENCE, not the drop.

Two channels, neither a realtime push:
  - the night-before job sheet, which re-sends as "Updated job sheet" on any
    content change and sends a "you're now clear" sheet to a crew member
    whose jobs all went away (verified in lib/crew-sheet/dispatch.ts);
  - a "Called off" card on /my-jobs.

Realtime crew-allocation pushes were retired the same day on Peter's call —
crew rely on the job sheet, not on live add/remove pings — so this fix
deliberately does NOT add one. See the follow-up PR retiring the crew_job
push category.

## Verify
