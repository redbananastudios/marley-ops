## Summary
The dashboard reports "SURVEYS BOOKED 2 · 100% of leads" for a cohort in which **no survey appointment exists at all** — the metric infers "surveyed" from lead status rank, so every lead that reaches `quoted`/`confirmed` counts as surveyed even when the survey stage was skipped entirely.

## Repro
1. Staging, 2026-08-20 (build 438dcb5): today's lead cohort is two QA-SENTINEL leads (one `quoted`, one `confirmed`). `appointments` contains **zero rows** for either lead (verified by service-role SQL: no appointment of any type for those lead ids).
2. Admin login → `/`. The funnel/tile shows "Surveys booked 2 · 100% of leads". Expected (naively): 0, since no survey was ever booked.

## Evidence
- `lib/dashboard/compute.ts:272-276`:
  `reached = (l, target) => (STATUS_RANK[l.status] ?? -1) >= (STATUS_RANK[target] ?? 99)`
  `isSurveyed = (l) => prog.surveyed.has(l.id) || reached(l, "survey_booked")` — the second arm marks any lead at or past `survey_booked` rank as surveyed regardless of appointments.
- SQL: 0 appointments for the cohort leads; render says 2 surveys booked (screenshot `shot-dashboard.png` in run artifacts).

## Suspected cause
Deliberate design ("status alone misses leads that skipped a stage" — the inference exists so stage-skippers don't vanish from later funnel stages), but it makes the SURVEY stage read 100% for quote-without-survey flows. That flow is real: Luke sometimes quotes before/without the survey (2026-08-10 changelog). The tile therefore can't distinguish "we surveyed everyone" from "we surveyed no one", which corrupts exactly the conversion signal the funnel exists to show.

## Why risky, not safe-fix
Rendered numbers match the code's documented intent — the defect is the metric's semantics, which feed performance/conversion reading. Whether the fix is a label change ("Reached survey stage"), a definition change (count actual survey appointments, like `lib/schedule/attended.ts` derives attendance), or intentional as-is needs Peter's call — the same class of decision as the 08-10 attended-vs-completed rule.

## Verify
After Peter's decision: for a cohort with N leads and 0 survey appointments, the survey tile shows whatever the chosen definition dictates (0 for appointment-derived; renamed label if inference stays) — assert tile value against `appointments where appt_type='survey'` for the cohort by SQL.
