## Summary
The office sees two different "what we're owed" headline figures: `/bookings` "Balance outstanding" (£5,100) and `/payments` Due tab "Owed right now"/"Due" (£5,550.00) disagree by exactly the ledger's unpaid 25% commitment money (£450) — each page is internally correct against its own formula, but they diverge whenever any unpaid commitment invoice exists, and the QA ledger's standing invariant ("/bookings Balance outstanding matches /payments Due exactly", verified 2026-08-20) only ever held because unpaid commitments were £0 that day.

## Repro
1. Role: admin (staging). Precondition: at least one accepted quote with a raised, unpaid commitment invoice (staging currently has quote `E2E-RRACE-COM-001`, lead `c3ec0428-83e3-4e86-8b72-1f1623a808d5`: deposit paid, `commitment_invoice_amount=450`, `commitment_paid_at=null`, no removal appointment).
2. Open `/bookings` → note the "Balance outstanding" stat.
3. Open `/payments?tab=due` → note "Owed right now" / "Due".
4. Expected (per the QA ledger's recorded invariant): identical to the penny.
5. Actual: `/bookings` = £5,100; `/payments` Due = £5,550.00 — a £450 gap.

## Evidence
SQL replica (exact reimplementation of `classifyBooking`/`owedNow`/`loadBookingRows` over the 9 in-queue bookings, run 2026-08-26T00:15–00:18Z, scraped twice ~3 min apart with stable values):
- Σ `owed.balance` = **5100.00** → `/bookings` renders "£5,100" (matches its own formula, `app/(dashboard)/bookings/page.tsx:217`, sums `owed.balance` only).
- Σ `owed.total` = **5550.00** → `/payments` Due renders "£5,550.00" (matches its own formula, `app/(dashboard)/payments/due-tab.tsx:147-148`, sums `owed.total` = commitment + balance).
- The whole gap is one row: `E2E-RRACE-COM-001` with unpaid commitment £450. `classifyBooking()` buckets it `no_date` (the commitment/balance ladder only runs once `hasRemovalAppt` is true, `lib/bookings/queue.ts:145-153`), but `owedNow()` is deliberately bucket-independent (its own doc comment: "a booking can owe the 25% AND the balance at once") and reports `owed.commitment=450`, which the Due tab counts and the /bookings stat (by its own name) does not.
- Every other money figure on both pages matched the SQL recompute exactly (deposits outstanding 8/£800 on both pages, all other buckets 0). Not a pennies/float bug — `round2` applied consistently on both paths.

## Suspected cause
Not a computation bug on either page — a semantic divergence. Either (a) `/bookings` "Balance outstanding" was always meant to exclude commitment money, in which case this is working-as-designed, the two figures are different quantities, and the 2026-08-20 QA-ledger invariant was a coincidence of a £0-commitment day (the ledger has been corrected this run to say so); or (b) the two headline figures were meant to stay in lockstep as "what customers owe us right now", in which case `/bookings` needs an `owed.total`-based stat (or a separate commitment line) — money-display semantics, so a decision for Peter, not automation. Filed `risky` because any fix touches money aggregation the office acts on.

## Resolution (2026-08-28, branch `qa/money-headlines-per-obligation`)

Answer to the (a)/(b) question above: **both, and neither page was the one at
fault.** The two headlines DO answer different questions by design, so the
2026-08-20 ledger invariant was never real - but the decomposition underneath
them did not reconcile, which is a genuine arithmetic bug.

`/bookings` computed its two money tiles on different bases: the balance tile
per OBLIGATION (`owed.balance`, already fixed this way for QA-20260820-04) and
the 25% tile per BUCKET (`commitment_overdue` + `commitment_due`). The bucket
ladder only reaches `commitment_*` once the deposit is paid AND a removal
appointment exists, while `ensureCommitmentInvoice` requires neither - only a
confirmed date and the customer's `date_confirm` signature
(`lib/quote/accept-flow.ts:1651-1702`), and `confirmMoveDate` creates no
appointment. So an invoiced, unpaid 25% sat unseen in `deposit_outstanding`,
`no_date` or `provisional` - counted by /payments, absent from the /bookings
tile that names it. Exactly the shape already fixed once for the balance.

Worse than the tile: the office's own "25% overdue - call them today" section
is bucket-driven too, so a booking could be genuinely date-releasable and red
on /payments while missing from the call-today list on /bookings.

**Fixed** by `queueMoney` in `lib/bookings/queue.ts` - one pure seam producing
every money headline on both pages per obligation, so they cannot diverge by
construction. Bucket membership now decides which LIST a row appears in and
nothing else.

**Also fixed, found while verifying:** a balance invoice raised EARLY by gate
9b (late booking) or gate 9c (settle in full) was reported as GBP 0 owed by
both headlines whenever the office had not yet allocated a slot, because
`owedNow` required `hasRemovalAppt` for every balance. Since 9b only fires for
moves already inside T-7, the jobs moving soonest were the ones reading zero.
An issued invoice is now owed on its own authority; a balance merely implied
by a date window still requires the appointment, which is what that guard was
written for.

**The invariant that replaces the false one**, unit-locked in
`tests/lib/bookings/queue.test.ts`:

1. /payments "Owed right now" = SUM(owed.commitment) + SUM(owed.balance).
2. "Overdue" + "Due" partitions it exactly.
3. /bookings "25% to collect" + "Balance to collect" = "Owed right now".
   **Neither tile alone equals it** - that was the ledger's mistake.
4. Deposits are disjoint from every owed figure.
5. Every figure is computed per obligation, never per bucket.
6. A small job (gate 9a) contributes zero for its whole life.
7. A raised-but-unpaid balance counts from the moment it is raised.

Labels corrected too: "Balance outstanding" -> "Balance to collect" (matching
its own section heading), and the due-tab docstring sentence that seeded the
false invariant now states what the shared seam does and does not promise.
