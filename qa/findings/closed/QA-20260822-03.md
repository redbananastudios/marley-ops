## Suspected cause
Two candidates, both unproven — the trace artifacts (`trace.zip`, uploaded by the run) are
the way to settle it, and were not opened during this pass:

- **(1) shared crew fixture, no teardown.** Both statement specs drive the *same* persistent
  `e2e-crew` fixture over the *same* pay period. `invoicing-submit-lines` explicitly deletes
  that crew's `contractor_agreements` + `staff_statements` in its own setup precisely to
  start clean (see its header comment at `:13-15`); the newer `hours-to-admin-statements`
  creates and **submits** a statement and cleans up nothing. A submitted statement for the
  current period would plausibly leave "start (or reuse the draft for) this week's invoice"
  with no draft to create or reuse, which matches failure 2's `undefined` locator exactly.
- **(2) failure 1 is its own bug.** The office readback seeing **0** rows (not >1) is not
  explained by leftovers. It is either a genuine handoff defect, a wrong row locator, or the
  office `storageState` not being valid in CI. Note the 12:09 audit proved this handoff
  **live and green by hand** (crew MMP031 -> office `/finance/statements`, 0 findings), which
  points at the spec rather than the product — but that is an inference, not a verification.

## Fix

Both hypotheses in `Suspected cause` were tested against the source before anything was
changed, and **both were spec bugs, not product bugs** — which matches the 12:09 audit
having proven this handoff live and green by hand.

**Failure 1 — the office readback saw 0 rows.** `components/finance/office-statements-view.tsx`
renders statement lines only once a card is EXPANDED: its own header says "expand to see the
lines", `expanded` initialises to an empty `Set`, and `{l.description}` sits inside the
`isOpen` branch. The spec filtered its locator on the line description, which is therefore
never in the DOM at that moment — it could only ever have found 0. The manual audit passed
because a human matched on crew name and total, which the COLLAPSED card does show.
Fixed by locating on crew name + total, then **expanding and asserting the description
anyway** — the point of the handoff is that the office reads the crew's line back
identically, not merely that an invoice for the right money exists, so the assertion is
kept and reached rather than dropped.

**Failure 2 — the sibling could not open a draft.** `invoicing-submit-lines.spec.ts`
documents relying on starting "statement-free". The seed does guarantee that, but only once
per CI invocation — and `hours-to-admin-statements.spec.ts` sorts first, creates this week's
invoice and **submits** it, so by the time the sibling clicks "This week" there is no
editable draft, and `submitUntil` times out with an undefined locator. With
`fullyParallel: false, workers: 1` this is a deterministic ordering collision, not a race.
Fixed with a `test.afterAll` teardown in the spec that made the mess (new
`deleteCrewStatements` in `e2e/fixtures/db.ts`), restoring the precondition the sibling
documents. Deliberately NOT fixed by loosening the sibling to tolerate a submitted
statement — that would erode a real assertion to accommodate test pollution.

Also corrected the spec's own header, which claimed it ships skipped "because this
environment has no E2E_CREW_PASSWORD/E2E_OFFICE_PASSWORD". CI has both, which is exactly why
it ran unvalidated and failed on its first outing. The guard is kept for local runs, where
those variables genuinely are unset, and the comment now says so.

**Failure 3 is untouched by design** — `job-detail.spec.ts` is the tracked
`waitForLoadState` flake ([ClickUp 869egr5w8](https://app.clickup.com/t/869egr5w8)), deflaked separately. It may still fail this
run; it has always passed on re-run.
